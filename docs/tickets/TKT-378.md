# TKT-378 — Round Result Payload And Player UI For Zone Status

- Typ: Applikation
- Priorität: P1
- Status: Planned

## Problem
Players currently do not receive clear visibility into their zone, whether local demand was covered, what the binding grid constraints were, or why additional network costs occurred.

## Ziel
Expose Phase 1 interzonal results in backend payloads and player-facing round results.

## Umsetzung
- Add `zone_results` and `link_results` to round payloads.
- Add `player_zone_info` to player round results.
- Display player zone badge in round results.
- Add a zone status card with:
  - local generation,
  - local demand,
  - imports,
  - exports,
  - unserved demand,
  - extra cost total,
  - extra cost per MWh.
- Add a compact link utilization table for the player's zone.
- Show `grid_constraint_cost_zar` as its own KPI and explanation item.

## Akzeptanzkriterien
- Every player can see their assigned zone in round results.
- Round results distinguish between local sufficiency, grid-supported sufficiency, and shortage.
- Link utilization for the player's zone is visible and understandable.
- Grid constraint cost is shown separately from imbalance.
